# 06 — Contribution Practice

Where reading becomes doing. Every ticket answers the maintainer's five questions: **why is it useful, why is the blast radius small, what existing pattern does it follow, how is it tested, and what would make a maintainer reject it.** No drive-by refactors.

Reality check: this is a widely-used *template* maintained by [jasontaylordev](../../README.md#L11). Real contributions must respect that its simplicity is a feature — a maintainer will reject anything that makes the scaffold heavier without clear payoff. Many tickets here are therefore framed as "do it in your fork to learn," with a note on whether it's plausibly upstreamable. Each ticket carries an **Interview story potential** line — the STAR story completing it would produce, feeding [08-interview-prep/06](../08-interview-prep/06-behavioral-star-stories.md).
