## How to read this register

Rows R1–R3 are the *architecturally significant* ones and the best interview material (they're each a full [senior project or mid-ticket](../06-contribution-practice/README.md)). R6–R10 are the *approachable* ones (junior tickets). R15–R16, R19 are labeled **Hypothesis** deliberately — [the prompt's rule](../README.md) is to never call something a bug without confirming it, and these are inspection-level suspicions, not demonstrated failures.

Reminder throughout: this is a *template*. R1, R2, R13 especially are scope decisions that make the template simpler to learn; they become genuine risks the moment someone deploys a fork to real users. Say it that carefully — the judgment to distinguish "wrong" from "deliberately out of scope" is itself the [senior skill being taught](../03-architecture-and-patterns/06-architecture-critique.md).
